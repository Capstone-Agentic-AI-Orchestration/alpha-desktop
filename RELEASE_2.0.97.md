# Alpha Desktop 2.0.97 — Cleaner workspace and smoother chat panels

- Workspace tabs sit directly in the title bar, and a curved border connects the top and left edges of the page.
- Double-click empty space on the sidebar or press Ctrl+B to open or close it. On wider windows it docks alongside the page.
- The chat conversation list and side panel slide open and closed when clicked. The animation respects reduced-motion settings.
- Redundant page headings were removed, Scan host engines moved into the Runtimes toolbar, and runtime details were simplified.
