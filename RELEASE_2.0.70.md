# Alpha Desktop 2.0.70

This release lets you approve builds in your own words and redesigns the Orchestrator bar.

- Replies to a pending plan or publication review are understood in context, so "yes, do it" or "I approve this plan" works without exact keywords. Plan approval and publication approval stay separate, and nothing runs unless the server confirms the current review, your permissions and a single pending decision.
- The Orchestrator answers through a structured response, which removes a hidden routing line that some models dropped or mangled.
- The collapsed Orchestrator bar spans the full chat width, shows the build's progress from Plan to Pull request, and rests with a slow animated wave when no build is open.
- Build checks are bounded, and planned tasks are grounded in the repository's actual scripts and setup.
